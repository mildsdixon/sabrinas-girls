Our mission

Reframing behavioral challenges as high-value leadership.

We take young women labeled as disruptive for their “gift of gab” and train them to channel that vocal energy into public speaking, professional communication, and youth leadership.

By teaching them how to use their voice with purpose, we turn classroom distractions into future executives, advocates, and community leaders.

“In most schools, a girl who talks too much gets a detention slip. At Sabrina’s Girls, she gets a microphone.”

The paradigm shift

Excessive talking isn’t a lack of discipline—it’s unguided leadership. Unchanneled vocal energy leads to disciplinary marks; guided vocal energy leads to public speaking, debate, and executive presence.

School engagement

Instead of pushing these expressive girls to the margins of the classroom, we give them the tools to command the room constructively, improving classroom focus and school climate.

What we do

Give her voice a purpose.

An 8-week intensive for high school young women.

01 / COMMUNICATION

Public Speaking & Communication Training

Core competencies in public speaking, professional etiquette, personal branding, and communication strategy.

02 / ADVOCACY

Conflict Resolution & Advocacy

Channeling natural vocal energy into purposeful communication and advocacy.

03 / LEADERSHIP

Leadership Development & Mentorship

Building the skills to become confident public speakers, debaters, and future leaders.

04 / EXPERIENCE

Real-World Practice & Executive Presence

Participants don’t just practice in a vacuum—they step up as keynote speakers and mentors for K–8 classrooms, immediately demonstrating the power of their voice to younger peers.

Skills-based curriculum

Our 8-week intensive equips high school young women with core competencies in public speaking, professional etiquette, personal branding, and communication strategy.

Confidence & polish

Through our structured workshops and culminating Glam Day, we build the poise, confidence, and self-worth necessary for these girls to step comfortably into leadership roles.
